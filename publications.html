Awards

Best Paper Award - INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON SMART MECHANICAL SYSTEMS FOR SUSTAINABILITY (ICSMSS 2025)

Dr./Mr./Ms. Rahul Kumbhar, Samir B. Kumbhar, Ranjit Anil Patil has been awarded the Best Paper Award for the paper titled Influence of Shape Memory Alloy (SMA) Wire Position and Transformation Temperature on the Natural Frequencies of SMA-Reinforced Composite Beams presented at the International Conference on Smart Mechanical Systems for Sustainability (ICSMSS 2025), organised by the Department of Mechanical Engineering, jointly with Indian Society of Manufacturing Engineers on 22nd and 23rd December 2025 at DJSCE, Mumbai, in recognition of its outstanding quality, originality, and technical contribution.

Promising Young Teacher Award

Ranjit A. Patil received the Promising Young Teacher Award 2023 in recognition of his valuable contributions to academia during the academic year 2022–23. The award was presented on 5 September 2023, on the occasion of Teacher’s Day, by the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE), RIT Chapter, in association with the Academic Section of Rajarambapu Institute of Technology, Rajaramnagar.
